## 2 · 00:35–01:45 · What `--backend mpc` actually is

**SCREEN** — Card, two boxes: *Earth Engine — account, key, quota* and
*Planetary Computer — open, anonymous*.

**VO (ID)** — Microsoft Planetary Computer menyimpan arsip Sentinel dan Landsat
dan membukanya tanpa pendaftaran. Untuk skenario optik dan radar, itu sudah
cukup — dan satu tanda saja yang membedakan.

**VO (EN)** — Microsoft Planetary Computer holds the Sentinel and Landsat
archives and serves them without registration. For the optical and radar
scenarios that is enough — and one flag is all it takes.

**LOWER-3** — `--backend mpc`

**VO (ID)** — Delapan skenario jalan begini: `deforestation`, `flood`, `burn`,
`water`, `urbanization`, `urban-trend`, `mining`, `disturbance`. Semuanya
berbasis citra Sentinel atau Landsat. Yang **tidak** bisa adalah yang butuh
cuaca, penduduk, atau titik panas — karena data itu tidak ada di katalognya.

**VO (EN)** — Eight scenarios work this way: `deforestation`, `flood`, `burn`,
`water`, `urbanization`, `urban-trend`, `mining`, `disturbance`. All of them are
built on Sentinel or Landsat imagery. What **cannot** work is anything needing
weather, population or hotspots — that data is not in the catalogue.

**NOTE** — Do not say "imagery works on MPC" as a blanket statement. The
`imagery` scenario itself and `coastline` are Earth Engine only; the backend
table on the documentation page is the authority. Getting this wrong sends
viewers into an error with no obvious cause.

## 3 · 01:45–03:35 · The command

**SCREEN** — Terminal. Type in full.

**TYPE**
```bash
earthchange -s deforestation --lat 1.0 --lon 102.5 --radius 6 -n Riau \
  --backend mpc \
  --pre 2019-01-01:2019-12-31 \
  --post 2023-01-01:2023-12-31
```

**VO (ID)** — Empat hal. Di mana — titik tengah dan radius. Backend-nya.
Jendela **pre**, garis dasar. Dan jendela **post**, keadaan sesudahnya.

**VO (EN)** — Four things. Where — a centre and a radius. The backend. The
**pre** window, your baseline. And the **post** window, the state afterwards.

**VO (ID)** — Yang dihitung adalah selisih NDVI antara dua jendela itu. NDVI
mengukur seberapa hijau dan seberapa rimbun vegetasi dari pantulan merah dan
inframerah-dekat. NDVI turun tajam berarti vegetasi hilang.

**VO (EN)** — What it computes is the NDVI difference between those two windows.
NDVI measures how green and how dense vegetation is, from red and near-infrared
reflectance. A sharp NDVI drop means vegetation lost.

**SCREEN** — Run it. Hold on the progress output.

**NOTE** — Say the real duration. This is a network-bound job pulling a year of
scenes at each end.

## 5 · 05:30–07:35 · Windows that are actually comparable

**SCREEN** — Card: two calendars, a full year highlighted on each.

**VO (ID)** — Kesalahan paling sering di sini bukan soal kode. Soal jendela.

**VO (EN)** — The commonest mistake here is not a coding mistake. It is a window
mistake.

**VO (ID)** — Bandingkan Maret 2019 dengan September 2023, dan Anda tidak
mengukur deforestasi. Anda mengukur musim. Di Indonesia selisih kehijauan antara
puncak musim hujan dan puncak kemarau bisa lebih besar daripada selisih akibat
pembukaan lahan.

**VO (EN)** — Compare March 2019 with September 2023 and you are not measuring
deforestation. You are measuring the season. In Indonesia the greenness
difference between peak wet and peak dry can be larger than the difference
land clearing makes.

**LOWER-3** — `Jendela beda musim = Anda mengukur musim, bukan perubahan`

**VO (ID)** — Tiga aturan sederhana. Pakai **jendela satu tahun penuh** di kedua
sisi — musimnya saling meniadakan. Kalau harus lebih pendek, pakai **bulan yang
sama** di kedua sisi. Dan pastikan tiap jendela mengumpulkan cukup citra: di
daerah berawan, satu bulan bisa hanya menyisakan dua atau tiga pengamatan
bersih.

**VO (EN)** — Three simple rules. Use a **full year** on each side — the seasons
cancel. If you must go shorter, use the **same months** on both sides. And make
sure each window collects enough scenes: in a cloudy region a single month can
leave you two or three clean observations.

**SCREEN** — Cut to the second run's stats, full screen.

**OUTPUT** ✓ *(from run `20260708-223128_deforestation_m3p333_122p25_a26cba`)*
```json
"results": {
    "mean": 0.0197,
    "pct_affected": 0.91,
    "pct_severe": 0.37,
    "scenes_pre": 51,
    "scenes_post": 26
}
```

**VO (ID)** — Ini run lain, di Sulawesi Tenggara. Rerata selisihnya **positif** —
sedikit lebih hijau, bukan lebih gundul. Kurang dari satu persen wilayah
terdampak.

**VO (EN)** — This is a different run, in Southeast Sulawesi. The mean difference
is **positive** — slightly greener, not cleared. Under one per cent of the area
affected.

**VO (ID)** — Inilah wujud hasil "tidak terjadi apa-apa", dan Anda perlu tahu
wujudnya. Kalau satu-satunya peta yang pernah Anda lihat adalah yang merah
menyala, Anda akan mulai membaca derau sebagai kejadian.

**VO (EN)** — This is what "nothing happened" looks like, and you need to know
what it looks like. If the only map you have ever seen is the bright red one,
you will start reading noise as an event.

**LOWER-3** — `Hasil nol juga hasil`

## 6 · 07:35–09:35 · Cloud, shadow, and `--min-obs`

**SCREEN** — Diagram: a cloud, its shadow on the ground, and the NDVI trace
dipping under the shadow.

**VO (ID)** — Awan dan bayangannya adalah sumber positif palsu terbesar di sini,
dan bayangan lebih berbahaya daripada awannya.

**VO (EN)** — Cloud and its shadow are the biggest source of false positives
here, and shadow is the more dangerous of the two.

**VO (ID)** — Awan itu terang dan mudah ditandai. Bayangan itu gelap — dan
bayangan menekan NDVI. Piksel yang tertutup bayangan terlihat persis seperti
piksel yang kehilangan vegetasi. Perhatikan: bayangan **selalu** meniru
kehilangan, tidak pernah meniru pertambahan. Jadi biasnya searah, dan arahnya
adalah arah yang Anda cari.

**VO (EN)** — Cloud is bright and easy to flag. Shadow is dark — and shadow
depresses NDVI. A shadowed pixel looks exactly like a pixel that lost
vegetation. Note this: shadow **always** mimics loss and never mimics gain. So
the bias is one-directional, and its direction is the direction you are
looking for.

**LOWER-3** — `Bayangan meniru kehilangan, tidak pernah pertambahan`

**VO (ID)** — Pertahanannya adalah jumlah pengamatan. Nilai tengah dari delapan
puluh citra tahan terhadap beberapa yang berbayang. Nilai tengah dari satu citra
**adalah** citra itu — termasuk bayangannya.

**VO (EN)** — The defence is observation count. A median of eighty scenes
survives a few shadowed ones. A median of one scene **is** that scene —
shadow and all.

**TYPE**
```bash
earthchange -s deforestation --lat 1.0 --lon 102.5 --radius 6 -n Riau \
  --backend mpc --pre 2019-01-01:2019-12-31 --post 2023-01-01:2023-12-31 \
  --min-obs 5
```

**VO (ID)** — `--min-obs` menyuruh run hanya melaporkan perubahan di piksel yang
punya minimal sekian pengamatan bersih di **kedua** jendela. Piksel yang tak
memenuhi syarat dikosongkan, bukan ditebak.

**VO (EN)** — `--min-obs` tells the run to report change only where a pixel has
at least that many clean observations in **both** windows. Pixels that fail are
left empty, not guessed.

**VO (ID)** — Lubang di peta Anda bukan cacat. Lubang itu jujur.

**VO (EN)** — Holes in your map are not a defect. The holes are the honest part.

## 7 · 09:35–11:35 · Flood — radar, and the one-scene problem

**SCREEN** — The Pekalongan flood map, full frame.

**VO (ID)** — Banjir tidak bisa dipetakan dengan citra optik, karena banjir
datang bersama awan. Jadi skenario ini pakai radar — Sentinel-1, yang menembus
awan dan bekerja malam hari. Air permukaan tampak gelap bagi radar, karena
memantulkan sinyal menjauh dari satelit.

**VO (EN)** — Flooding cannot be mapped with optical imagery, because flooding
arrives with cloud. So this scenario uses radar — Sentinel-1, which sees through
cloud and works at night. Standing water looks dark to radar, because it
reflects the signal away from the satellite.

**TYPE**
```bash
earthchange -s flood --lat -6.8831 --lon 109.6597 --radius 12 -n Pekalongan \
  --backend mpc \
  --pre 2025-07-01:2025-12-31 \
  --post 2026-01-01:2026-01-27
```

**OUTPUT** ✓ *(from run `20260709-182323_flood_pekalongan_jan2026_mpc_79dbe0`)*
```json
"results": {
    "method": "SAR water (VV, MPC), permanent-water & ocean masked",
    "orbit": "descending",
    "pct_flooded": 3.37,
    "pct_water_masked": 42.71,
    "scenes_pre": 10,
    "scenes_post": 1
}
```

**VO (ID)** — Tiga koma empat persen tergenang. Tapi lihat dua angka lainnya
dulu.

**VO (EN)** — Three point four per cent flooded. But look at the other two
numbers first.

**VO (ID)** — Empat puluh dua persen wilayah ini **di-mask** — laut dan air
permanen. Jadi tiga koma empat persen itu dihitung dari daratan yang tersisa,
bukan dari seluruh lingkaran. Pekalongan di pesisir; kalau angka mask ini tidak
dibaca, laporan Anda salah penyebut.

**VO (EN)** — Forty-two per cent of this area is **masked** — sea and permanent
water. So the three point four per cent is of the land that remains, not of the
whole circle. Pekalongan is coastal; miss that masked figure and your report has
the wrong denominator.

**VO (ID)** — Dan `scenes_post` bernilai **satu**. Satu lintasan radar. Itu bukan
kesalahan — banjir memang kejadian sesaat, dan Sentinel-1 lewat setiap beberapa
hari. Tapi artinya peta ini adalah potret satu momen, bukan rerata. Kalau
airnya surut sehari sebelum lintasan itu, peta ini tidak melihatnya.

**VO (EN)** — And `scenes_post` is **one**. A single radar pass. That is not an
error — a flood is a moment, and Sentinel-1 comes round every few days. But it
means this map is one instant, not an average. If the water drained the day
before that pass, this map never saw it.

**LOWER-3** — `scenes_post: 1 — potret, bukan rerata`

**NOTE** — This is the most important habit the episode teaches: read
`scenes_pre` and `scenes_post` before reading the headline percentage. Deliver
it slowly.

## 8 · 11:35–13:00 · Why the two backends will not agree to the pixel

**SCREEN** — Two versions of the same map side by side, MPC and Earth Engine,
similar but not identical.

**VO (ID)** — Kalau nanti Anda punya akun Earth Engine dan menjalankan analisis
yang sama di kedua backend, hasilnya akan mirip tapi tidak sama persis. Itu
diharapkan, dan sebaiknya Anda tahu alasannya sebelum ada yang menanyakannya.

**VO (EN)** — When you eventually have an Earth Engine account and run the same
analysis on both backends, the results will be close but not identical. That is
expected, and you should know why before somebody asks.

**VO (ID)** — Katalognya tidak persis sama isinya. Masker awannya berbeda
implementasi. Penyusunan ulang piksel dan grid keluarannya berbeda. Tak satu pun
dari ini "bug" — dan tak satu pun dari keduanya adalah kebenaran.

**VO (EN)** — The catalogues do not hold exactly the same scenes. The cloud masks
are different implementations. The resampling and the output grid differ. None
of that is a bug — and neither one of them is the truth.

**VO (ID)** — Aturan praktisnya: **pilih satu backend untuk satu laporan.**
Kalau Anda membandingkan dua kabupaten, atau dua tahun, jalankan keduanya dengan
backend yang sama. Kalau Anda mencampur, selisih backend akan masuk ke dalam
temuan Anda dan Anda tidak akan bisa memisahkannya lagi.

**VO (EN)** — The practical rule: **pick one backend per report.** If you are
comparing two districts, or two years, run both on the same backend. Mix them and
the backend difference walks into your finding, and you will not be able to
separate it out again.

**LOWER-3** — `Satu laporan, satu backend`

## 9 · 13:00–14:00 · Now do your own

**SCREEN** — Terminal, the command with the coordinates selected.

**VO (ID)** — Sekarang giliran wilayah Anda. Ganti koordinatnya, pakai jendela
satu tahun penuh di kedua sisi, dan mulailah dengan radius kecil — sepuluh
kilometer — supaya cepat selesai. Perbesar setelah Anda yakin jendelanya benar.

**VO (EN)** — Now your own area. Change the coordinates, use full-year windows on
both sides, and start with a small radius — ten kilometres — so it finishes
quickly. Scale up once you are confident the windows are right.

**TYPE**
```bash
earthchange -s deforestation --city 'Palangkaraya, Indonesia' --radius 10 \
  -n uji --backend mpc \
  --pre 2022-01-01:2022-12-31 --post 2025-01-01:2025-12-31
```

**VO (ID)** — Sampai di sini, tanpa satu pun akun, Anda sudah bisa membuat
animasi asap dan peta perubahan. Untuk lima belas skenario sisanya — cuaca,
kekeringan, titik panas, penduduk — kita perlu kredensial. Itu episode
berikutnya, dan itu satu-satunya episode yang membosankan di seri ini.

**VO (EN)** — At this point, with no account at all, you can make a smoke
animation and a change map. For the other fifteen scenarios — weather, drought,
hotspots, population — we need credentials. That is the next episode, and it is
the only boring one in this series.

## Social pull (60 s vertical)

The `scenes_post: 1` beat. The flood map, then the JSON, then the line
*"potret, bukan rerata"*. It is a piece of judgement no dashboard shows you, it
takes forty seconds, and it makes a viewer better at reading everyone else's
flood maps too.

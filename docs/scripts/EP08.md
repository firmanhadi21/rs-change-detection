# Episode 8 · Finding today's usable date
### *Menemukan tanggal yang bisa dipakai hari ini*

**Runtime** 9:00 · **Prerequisite** EP05

**Aim** — Make the viewer independent of every date written in these videos.

**Deliverable** — A habit, not a file.

> Format legend and production rules: [README.md](README.md).
>
> **This is the episode that keeps the other eleven usable as they age.** Every
> date in this series is already stale by the time anybody watches it. Rather
> than pretend otherwise, this episode teaches the viewer to derive their own.

---

## 2 · 00:35–01:50 · Six archives, six different end dates

**SCREEN** — `GFX-LAG`: six horizontal timelines stacked, all ending at
different points, with "hari ini" as a vertical line on the right.

**VO (ID)** — Tidak ada satu pun "data terbaru". Ada enam arsip, dan tiap satu
berhenti di hari yang berbeda.

**VO (EN)** — There is no such thing as "the latest data". There are six archives,
and each one stops on a different day.

**NOTE** ✓ — Measured on **11 August 2026**. Put that date on screen beside the
graphic and say it out loud. These are not constants — they are a snapshot, and
the whole point of the episode is that the viewer measures their own.

**SCREEN** — Each bar labelled as narrated:

```
diukur 2026-08-11

FIRMS titik panas         → 2026-08-10     ( 1 hari )
GDAS1 meteorologi         → 2026-08-07     ( 4 hari, terbit mingguan )
ERA5 / ERA5-Land jam-jaman→ 2026-08-04     ( 7 hari )
ERA5-Land harian          → 2026-08-02     ( 9 hari )
CAMS kualitas udara       → ke DEPAN       ( prakiraan )
MODIS luas terbakar       → ± 100 hari     ( ~3 bulan )
```

**VO (ID)** — Dari satu hari sampai tiga bulan. Dan satu di antaranya justru
berjalan ke **depan**, karena CAMS adalah prakiraan, bukan pengamatan.

**VO (EN)** — From one day to three months. And one of them runs **forward**,
because CAMS is a forecast, not an observation.

**VO (ID)** — Jadi pertanyaannya bukan "data terbaru sampai kapan". Pertanyaannya:
**hari terakhir mana yang tercakup oleh setiap sumber yang dibutuhkan run saya.**

**VO (EN)** — So the question is not "how recent is the data". The question is:
**what is the last day covered by every source my run needs.**

**LOWER-3** — `Bukan "data terbaru" — "hari terakhir yang tercakup semuanya"`

## 3 · 01:50–03:35 · Refusal one — the window runs past the end

**SCREEN** — Terminal, full frame, the refusal held long enough to read twice.

**OUTPUT** ✓ *(the form produced by `require_span`)*
```
CHIRPS daily rainfall does not cover the whole window.
  needed:    2026-05-14 to 2026-08-11
  available: 1981-01-01 to 2026-07-06

End the window on 2026-07-06 or earlier.

Reanalysis runs several days behind real time. For the last few days use
smoke-video, which reads live feeds and needs no Earth Engine.
```

**VO (ID)** — Perhatikan apa yang dilakukan pesan ini. Ia tidak bilang "data
tidak ditemukan". Ia menyebutkan apa yang Anda minta, apa yang ada, dan
**tanggal persis yang harus Anda pakai.**

**VO (EN)** — Look at what this message does. It does not say "data not found".
It states what you asked for, what exists, and **the exact date you should use
instead.**

**VO (ID)** — Anda tidak perlu memahami CHIRPS untuk melanjutkan. Salin
tanggalnya, jalankan lagi. Selesai.

**VO (EN)** — You do not need to understand CHIRPS to move on. Copy the date, run
it again. Done.

**VO (ID)** — Dan itu bukan kebetulan. Pesan kesalahan yang cuma bilang "gagal"
memaksa Anda jadi ahli sebelum boleh bekerja. Pesan yang menyebutkan langkah
berikutnya tidak.

**VO (EN)** — And that is not an accident. An error that only says "failed" makes
you become an expert before you may work. One that names your next move does not.

## 4 · 03:35–05:35 · Refusal two — the one that matters

**SCREEN** — The second refusal, full frame.

**OUTPUT** ✓ *(the form produced by `require_hours`)*
```
ERA5 100 m wind holds 48 hourly images where this run needs 49.
  window: 2026-08-04 00:00 to 2026-08-06 01:00

A near-real-time reanalysis ingests its most recent day a few hours at a
time, so the range looks covered while hours inside it are missing.

The last complete day is 2026-08-03. End the run on or before it.
```

**VO (ID)** — Ini yang lebih halus, dan ini yang paling penting Anda pahami.

**VO (EN)** — This is the subtle one, and the one most worth understanding.

**VO (ID)** — Reanalisis waktu-nyaris-nyata tidak masuk sekaligus per hari. Ia
masuk beberapa jam sekali. Jadi pada suatu saat, arsipnya bisa punya cap waktu
terakhir pukul tujuh malam tanggal 4 Agustus — dan cuma memuat **dua** dari dua
puluh empat jam hari itu.

**VO (EN)** — A near-real-time reanalysis does not arrive a whole day at a time.
It arrives a few hours at a time. So at any moment the archive can have a last
timestamp of seven in the evening on the fourth of August — and hold **two** of
that day's twenty-four hours.

**LOWER-3** — `Cap waktu terakhir ≠ hari yang lengkap`

**VO (ID)** — Pemeriksaan rentang biasa akan lolos. Rentangnya memang tercakup.
Lalu run-nya gagal di tengah jalan, dengan pesan tentang unduhan GeoTIFF yang
tidak menyebut tanggal sama sekali — dan Anda akan menghabiskan sore mencari bug
yang tidak ada.

**VO (EN)** — An ordinary range check passes. The range *is* covered. Then the run
fails halfway through, with a message about a GeoTIFF download that never
mentions dates at all — and you spend an afternoon hunting a bug that does not
exist.

**VO (ID)** — Karena itu run ini **menghitung** jam yang ada, bukan memercayai
cap waktu terakhirnya. Empat puluh delapan yang tersedia, empat puluh sembilan
yang dibutuhkan — dan ia berhenti sebelum apa pun dikerjakan.

**VO (EN)** — Which is why this run **counts** the hours it has, rather than
trusting the last timestamp. Forty-eight available, forty-nine needed — and it
stops before doing any work.

**NOTE** — This refusal exists because that exact failure happened during
development: ERA5 held 2 of 24 hours on 4 August 2026 while its maximum
timestamp read 19:00. Tell that story; it is short and it makes the check
memorable.

## 5 · 05:35–06:55 · The two odd ones

**SCREEN** — `GFX-LAG` again, with the CAMS bar and the MODIS bar highlighted in
turn.

**VO (ID)** — Dua arsip berperilaku berbeda dari yang lain, dan keduanya bisa
menjebak.

**VO (EN)** — Two archives behave unlike the rest, and both can catch you out.

**VO (ID)** — **CAMS berjalan ke depan.** Ia model prakiraan, jadi ia punya data
untuk besok. Artinya skenario asap tidak akan pernah menolak karena tanggalnya
terlalu baru — dan artinya Anda bisa dengan tidak sengaja melaporkan prakiraan
sebagai pengamatan. Kalau tanggal Anda melewati hari ini, katakan dalam laporan
bahwa itu prakiraan.

**VO (EN)** — **CAMS runs ahead.** It is a forecast model, so it has data for
tomorrow. Which means the smoke scenarios will never refuse for being too
recent — and which means you can accidentally report a forecast as an
observation. If your date is past today, say so in the report.

**VO (ID)** — **MODIS luas terbakar tertinggal sekitar tiga bulan.** Ini yang
mengejutkan orang. Anda bisa menghitung bahaya kebakaran untuk minggu lalu, tapi
luas terbakarnya tidak akan ada sampai musim gugur. Dalam catatan musim
kebakaran, kolom "terbakar" untuk bulan-bulan terakhir akan kosong — dan kosong
di situ berarti "belum terbit", bukan "tidak ada yang terbakar".

**VO (EN)** — **MODIS burned area lags about three months.** This one surprises
people. You can compute fire danger for last week, but its burned area will not
exist until autumn. In a fire-season record the burned column for the most recent
months will be empty — and empty there means "not published yet", not "nothing
burned".

**LOWER-3** — `Kosong ≠ nol`

## 6 · 06:55–08:15 · Picking one `--end` for a whole chain

**SCREEN** — The six bars, with a vertical line dragged left until it sits under
all of them.

**VO (ID)** — Kalau Anda menjalankan satu skenario, ikuti saja tanggal yang
disebutkan pesan penolakannya. Kalau Anda menjalankan rantai lengkap — episode
sepuluh — satu `--end` menggerakkan semuanya, dan tanggal itu harus memuaskan
sumber yang paling lambat.

**VO (EN)** — If you are running one scenario, just take the date its refusal
names. If you are running the full chain — episode 10 — one `--end` drives
everything, and that date has to satisfy the slowest source.

**VO (ID)** — Cara praktisnya, dan ini kebiasaan yang saya sarankan: **jalankan
langkah yang paling rewel lebih dulu, kecil-kecilan.** Untuk rantai kebakaran,
itu `smoke-track`, karena ia butuh jam-jaman ERA5 yang lengkap.

**VO (EN)** — The practical method, and this is the habit I recommend: **run the
fussiest step first, small.** For the fire chain that is `smoke-track`, because it
needs complete hourly ERA5.

**TYPE**
```bash
earthchange -s smoke-track --lat -1.7 --lon 110.5 --radius 10 -n cek \
  --date 2026-08-11 --track-hours 48
```

**VO (ID)** — Ia akan menolak dan menyebut hari lengkap terakhirnya. Pakai
tanggal itu sebagai `--end` untuk seluruh rantai, dan tidak ada langkah lain yang
akan gagal karena tanggal.

**VO (EN)** — It will refuse and name its last complete day. Use that date as
`--end` for the whole chain, and no other step will fail on dates.

**VO (ID)** — Dan perhatikan: `--track-hours` ikut menentukan. Jendela empat
puluh delapan jam butuh empat puluh delapan jam yang lengkap. Naikkan jadi tujuh
puluh dua dan tanggal yang bisa dipakai mundur satu hari lagi.

**VO (EN)** — And note: `--track-hours` is part of the sum. A forty-eight-hour
window needs forty-eight complete hours. Raise it to seventy-two and your usable
date moves back another day.

**LOWER-3** — `Tanggal terikat = --end ditambah --track-hours, tercakup penuh`

## 7 · 08:15–09:00 · The habit

**SCREEN** — Presenter, or a plain card.

**VO (ID)** — Tidak ada berkas yang dihasilkan episode ini. Yang dihasilkan satu
kebiasaan, dan bunyinya begini.

**VO (EN)** — This episode produces no file. It produces one habit, and it goes
like this.

**VO (ID)** — Jangan pernah menghafal tanggal. Termasuk tanggal-tanggal di video
ini — semuanya sudah basi saat Anda menontonnya, dan memang seharusnya begitu.
Jalankan yang paling rewel dulu, kecil, biarkan ia menolak, baca tanggal yang
disebutkannya, lalu jalankan yang sungguhan.

**VO (EN)** — Never memorise a date. Including every date in this series — they
were all stale by the time you watched, and they were always going to be. Run the
fussiest one first, small, let it refuse, read the date it names, then run the
real one.

**VO (ID)** — Penolakan bukan kegagalan. Itu satu-satunya bagian dari sistem ini
yang tahu tanggal berapa hari ini.

**VO (EN)** — A refusal is not a failure. It is the only part of this system that
knows what today's date is.

**LOWER-3** — `Biarkan ia menolak. Penolakannya berisi jawabannya.`

**Aim** — Give the viewer an accurate mental model of the package before they
install anything.

## 2 · 00:48–01:35 · The gap this sits in

**SCREEN** — Presenter to camera, or a static card if narrating only. Behind:
a stock screenshot of a scientific-Python notebook, 60 cells deep, scrolled fast.

**VO (ID)** — Kalau Anda staf BPBD, analis LSM, atau wartawan lingkungan,
pertanyaan Anda biasanya sederhana. *Sekering apa kabupaten kami bulan ini?
Siapa yang menghirup asapnya?* Yang tidak sederhana adalah jalan menuju
jawabannya: pilih citra, pilih indeks, tulis masker awan, urus proyeksi, bikin
peta. Itu pekerjaan berminggu-minggu — dan pekerjaan itu diulang lagi dari nol
tiap kali pertanyaannya muncul lagi.

**VO (EN)** — If you work at a provincial disaster agency, an NGO, or a
newsroom, your question is usually simple. *How dry is our district this month?
Who breathed the smoke?* What is not simple is the road to the answer: choose
imagery, choose an index, write a cloud mask, handle the projection, make a map.
That is weeks of work — and it is redone from nothing every time the question
comes back.

**LOWER-3** — `Pertanyaannya sederhana. Jalannya yang tidak.`

## 3 · 01:35–02:55 · What a scenario is

**SCREEN** — Clean terminal, large font. Type slowly enough to read.

**TYPE**
```bash
earthchange -s fire-danger --admin Ketapang --date 2019-09-30
```

**VO (ID)** — Ini yang disebut *skenario*. Satu skenario adalah satu pertanyaan,
bukan satu algoritma. `fire-danger` bukan nama metode — itu nama pertanyaan:
*seberapa berbahaya kondisinya hari ini?* Metodenya sudah dipilihkan, dan bisa
Anda periksa. Yang Anda ubah cuma dua: **di mana**, dan **kapan**.

**VO (EN)** — This is a *scenario*. One scenario is one question, not one
algorithm. `fire-danger` is not the name of a method — it is the name of a
question: *how dangerous are conditions today?* The method is already chosen,
and you can go and check it. You change two things: **where**, and **when**.

**SCREEN** — Cursor moves up, edits the same line in place. `Ketapang` →
`"Kalimantan Tengah"`, `2019-09-30` → `2026-08-01`.

**TYPE**
```bash
earthchange -s fire-danger --admin "Kalimantan Tengah" --date 2026-08-01
```

**VO (ID)** — Perintah kedua ini analisis yang sama persis, tempat lain, tahun
lain. Tidak ada satu baris kode pun yang ditulis di antara keduanya.

**VO (EN)** — That second command is the identical analysis, somewhere else,
another year. Not one line of code was written between them.

**LOWER-3** — `Satu skenario = satu pertanyaan · Anda ganti: di mana, kapan`

**NOTE** — Do not run either one here. Episode 1 installs nothing. If the
presenter runs it and it works, the viewer assumes an account is required and
switches off; if it fails, we have opened the series on an error. Type, discuss,
move on.

## 4 · 02:55–04:20 · Twenty-four of them

**SCREEN** — Full-frame terminal.

**TYPE**
```bash
earthchange --list
```

**OUTPUT** ✓ *(verbatim — hold on screen, scroll once, slowly)*
```
Available scenarios (-s):

  deforestation  Deforestation — vegetation loss (Sentinel-2 NDVI)
  mining         Mining — radar temporal (SIRAD) + NDVI loss (S1 + S2)
  urbanization   Urbanisation — built-up gain (Sentinel-2 NDBI)
  ...
  drought        Drought — rainfall deficit (CHIRPS), vegetation health ...
  fire-history   Fire history — burned area per year, recurrence map ...
  population-change Population change — two GHSL epochs, gained/lost/present ...
```

**VO (ID)** — Dua puluh empat skenario. Perhatikan namanya: `deforestation`,
`flood`, `drought`, `smoke-exposure`. Semuanya nama pertanyaan. Tidak ada
skenario bernama "NDVI", padahal NDVI dipakai di dalam beberapa di antaranya.

**VO (EN)** — Twenty-four of them. Look at the names: `deforestation`, `flood`,
`drought`, `smoke-exposure`. Every one is the name of a question. There is no
scenario called "NDVI", even though NDVI is used inside several of them.

**SCREEN** — Cut to `GFX-CHAIN` reduced to three grouped columns.

**VO (ID)** — Ada tiga keluarga besar. **Bahaya** — apakah kondisinya
mendukung? kering, berbahaya, panas. **Dampak** — apa yang sudah terjadi?
hutan hilang, banjir, area terbakar, pantai mundur. Dan **paparan serta
pertanggungjawaban** — siapa yang terkena, dan kawasan siapa itu. Keluarga
ketiga inilah yang biasanya tidak ada di perangkat lain, dan justru itu yang
membuat hasilnya bisa diserahkan ke orang lain.

**VO (EN)** — Three families. **Hazard** — are conditions favourable? dry,
dangerous, hot. **Impact** — what already happened? forest lost, flooding, area
burned, shoreline retreat. And **exposure and accountability** — who was
affected, and whose designated area was it. The third family is the one usually
missing elsewhere, and it is the one that makes a result something you can hand
to somebody.

**LOWER-3** — `Bahaya · Dampak · Paparan & pertanggungjawaban`

## 5 · 04:20–05:45 · Which ones need an account

**SCREEN** — `GFX-BACKEND`, the three-column split, built one column at a time.
Then cut to the backend table on the documentation page in a browser.

**VO (ID)** — Sebelum Anda mendaftar apa pun: sembilan dari dua puluh empat
skenario tidak butuh akun Earth Engine sama sekali. `smoke-video` tidak butuh
apa-apa. Delapan lainnya jalan di Microsoft Planetary Computer dengan
`--backend mpc`.

**VO (EN)** — Before you sign up for anything: nine of the twenty-four need no
Earth Engine account at all. `smoke-video` needs nothing. Eight more run on
Microsoft Planetary Computer with `--backend mpc`.

**LOWER-3** — `Tanpa akun: 9 · Perlu Earth Engine: 15`

**VO (ID)** — Sisanya, lima belas, memang butuh akun — dan alasannya bukan
karena belum sempat dikerjakan. ERA5-Land, CAMS, FIRMS, MODIS, WorldPop, CHIRPS
dan batas administrasi GAUL tidak ada di katalog Planetary Computer. Itu batas
katalog, bukan porting yang belum selesai. Kalau data cuaca dan penduduk suatu
saat muncul di sana, skenario-skenario itu ikut pindah.

**VO (EN)** — The other fifteen do need an account — and not because somebody
has not got round to it. ERA5-Land, CAMS, FIRMS, MODIS, WorldPop, CHIRPS and the
GAUL administrative boundaries are not in Planetary Computer's catalogue. That
is a catalogue limit, not an unfinished port. If weather and population ever
land there, those scenarios follow.

**NOTE** — Say the next line plainly; it prevents the single most common support
question in episode 4.

**VO (ID)** — Dan satu hal: **tidak ada peralihan otomatis**. Kalau skenario
butuh Earth Engine dan kredensial Anda tidak ada, ia berhenti dan bilang begitu.
Ia tidak diam-diam pindah ke sumber lain dan memberi Anda angka yang berbeda
dari yang Anda kira.

**VO (EN)** — And one thing: **there is no automatic fallback**. If a scenario
needs Earth Engine and your credentials are missing, it stops and says so. It
does not quietly switch to a different source and hand you a number that is not
the one you think it is.

## 6 · 05:45–07:00 · Where this series is going

**SCREEN** — `GFX-CHAIN`, full, animating link by link in time with the
narration. Each link flashes the thumbnail of the real figure from
`output/chain/` as it lands.

**VO (ID)** — Skenario-skenario ini punya urutan sebab-akibat, dan itulah
tulang punggung seri ini. **Kering** — hujan Ketapang 74 persen dari normal.
**Jadi berbahaya** — Drought Code 259, kelas Tinggi. **Terbakar** — dua ribu
lima ratus tiga puluh titik panas di Area Penggunaan Lain saja. **Asap pergi.**
**Asap mendarat** — di Pontianak. **Dan melintas batas.**

**VO (EN)** — These scenarios have a causal order, and that order is the spine
of this series. **It dried** — Ketapang's rain at 74 per cent of normal. **It
became dangerous** — Drought Code 259, class High. **It burned** — two thousand
five hundred and thirty hotspots in one land designation alone. **The smoke
left.** **The smoke landed** — on Pontianak. **And it crossed a border.**

**NOTE** ✓ — All five figures are from the 9 August 2026 Ketapang chain run:
rain 74.17% of normal, DC 258.8, 2,530 hotspots in AREA PENGGUNAAN LAIN,
Pontianak carrying 4,221,570 of 4,693,780 unhealthy person-days. Do not round
"74 per cent" to "three-quarters" — the whole point is that these are checkable.

**VO (ID)** — Perhatikan langkah kelima. Kebakarannya di Ketapang. Yang
menghirupnya, hampir seluruhnya, di Pontianak — kabupaten yang berbeda. Kalimat
itu tidak bisa ditulis oleh peta kebakaran saja.

**VO (EN)** — Watch the fifth link. The fires are in Ketapang. The people who
breathed it are, almost entirely, in Pontianak — a different district. A fire map
on its own cannot write that sentence.

**LOWER-3** — `Yang terbakar dan yang menghirup: dua tempat berbeda`

## 7 · 07:00–08:00 · How to use this series

**SCREEN** — A simple card: the twelve episode titles, with 2, 3 and 8 marked
"tanpa akun".

**VO (ID)** — Anda tidak harus menonton semuanya berurutan. Episode dua dan tiga
menghasilkan sesuatu yang nyata tanpa akun apa pun — mulailah dari sana. Episode
empat baru mengurus kredensial, setelah ada yang membuatnya sepadan. Lima sampai
sembilan adalah musim kebakaran, dari kekeringan sampai paparan. Sepuluh dan
sebelas menyatukannya jadi satu dokumen yang bisa diserahkan.

**VO (EN)** — You do not have to watch these in order. Episodes 2 and 3 produce
something real with no account at all — start there. Episode 4 handles
credentials, once there is something to make it worth the trouble. Five to nine
are the fire season, from drought through exposure. Ten and eleven assemble it
into one document you can hand over.

**SCREEN** — Hold on episode 12's title alone.

**VO (ID)** — Dan episode dua belas soal mengatakan apa yang **tidak**
ditunjukkan angka Anda. Itu bukan penutup sopan. Kalau pekerjaan Anda akan
dibantah — dan kalau menyangkut kawasan hutan dan asap, pasti dibantah — episode
itulah yang membuat Anda bertahan.

**VO (EN)** — And episode 12 is about saying what your number does **not** show.
That is not a polite closing note. If your work is going to be challenged — and
where forest designation and haze are concerned, it will be — that episode is the
one that lets you hold your ground.

**LOWER-3** — `Ep. 2 berikutnya: animasi asap, tanpa akun, satu perintah`

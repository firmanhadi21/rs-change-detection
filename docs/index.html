Satellite Change Detection

 pip install earthchange

A hands-on tutorial. Map deforestation, mining, urbanisation, floods, burns and surface-water change anywhere on Earth from free satellite data — then export georeferenced GeoTIFFs and print-ready maps. All in Python, powered by Google Earth Engine and Microsoft Planetary Computer.

Tutorial praktis. Petakan deforestasi, tambang, urbanisasi, banjir, kebakaran, dan perubahan air permukaan di mana saja dari data satelit gratis — lalu ekspor GeoTIFF tergeoreferensi dan peta siap cetak. Semua dengan Python, ditenagai Google Earth Engine dan Microsoft Planetary Computer.

Why this existsMengapa perangkat lunak ini ada

Satellite data has been free for years. The ability to use it has not. The imagery is there — Sentinel, Landsat, MODIS, decades of it, downloadable by anyone. What stands between those images and an answer is a stack of things that take months to learn: cloud masking, projections, which index suits which question, what threshold deserves to be called "change", and how to turn a raster into a number someone can act on. Yet the people who most need the answer — a district forestry officer, an NGO researcher, a journalist on deadline, a lecturer preparing a class, a student with three months left — are often precisely the people without those months. So the question this package asks is a narrow one: can a single command do the whole thing?

Data satelit sudah gratis bertahun-tahun. Kemampuan memakainya belum. Citranya ada — Sentinel, Landsat, MODIS, berpuluh tahun, bisa diunduh siapa saja. Yang berdiri di antara citra itu dan sebuah jawaban adalah tumpukan hal yang perlu berbulan-bulan untuk dikuasai: penapisan awan, sistem proyeksi, indeks mana untuk pertanyaan mana, ambang berapa yang pantas disebut "berubah", dan bagaimana mengubah raster menjadi angka yang bisa ditindaklanjuti orang. Sementara mereka yang paling membutuhkan jawabannya — penyuluh kehutanan di kabupaten, peneliti lembaga swadaya, wartawan yang dikejar tenggat, dosen yang menyiapkan kuliah, mahasiswa yang tinggal punya tiga bulan — justru sering kali adalah orang-orang yang tidak punya bulan-bulan itu. Maka pertanyaan yang diajukan paket ini sempit saja: bisakah satu baris perintah mengerjakan seluruhnya?

ObjectivesTujuan

  • One command, one question. No Earth Engine scripting, no threshold to choose, no projections to manage.
  • Calibrated where it runs. Fire danger classes follow BMKG, not Canada. Day-length factors use the equatorial convention, because the standard tables were drawn up for 46°N. Drought Code leads over FWI, because peat fire is driven by deep drying. Air quality uses ISPU. Global tools are calibrated for everywhere, which means for nowhere in particular.
  • Outputs you can actually use. A print-ready A4 map sheet, a GeoTIFF that opens straight in QGIS, statistics as JSON, and text in Indonesian and English.
  • Your own data, not only global layers. Rice-field boundaries, forest designation, an authoritative peat map — crossed with public satellite data. That is the difference between "West Kalimantan is dry" and "87% of this nature reserve is dry".
  • Honest about its limits. Every output states what it cannot tell you. A tool that hides its limits misleads precisely when it is trusted most.
  • Free, open and citable. MIT licence, Zenodo DOI.
  • Satu perintah untuk satu pertanyaan. Tidak perlu menulis skrip Earth Engine, tidak perlu memilih ambang, tidak perlu mengurus proyeksi.
  • Dikalibrasi di tempat ia dipakai. Ambang bahaya kebakaran mengikuti BMKG, bukan Kanada. Faktor panjang hari memakai konvensi ekuatorial, karena tabel bakunya disusun untuk 46°LU. Drought Code dipimpinkan di atas FWI, karena kebakaran gambut didorong pengeringan lapisan dalam. Kualitas udara memakai ISPU. Perkakas global dikalibrasi untuk seluruh dunia — yang berarti tidak untuk tempat mana pun secara khusus.
  • Keluaran yang benar-benar bisa dipakai. Lembar peta A4 siap cetak, GeoTIFF yang langsung terbuka di QGIS, statistik JSON, teks dwibahasa.
  • Data Anda sendiri, bukan hanya data global. Lahan baku sawah, batas fungsi kawasan, peta gambut resmi — disilangkan dengan data satelit publik. Inilah yang membedakan "Kalimantan Barat kering" dari "87% cagar alam ini kering".
  • Jujur tentang batasnya. Tiap keluaran menyebutkan apa yang tidak bisa ia katakan. Perangkat yang menyembunyikan batasnya akan menyesatkan tepat pada saat ia paling dipercaya.
  • Gratis, terbuka, dapat disitasi. Lisensi MIT, DOI Zenodo.

The hopeHarapan

The measure of this package is not downloads. It is whether an analysis that would never have happened, happens — because the person who needed to answer the question no longer had to become a remote sensing specialist first. A lecturer teaches with it. A student finishes a thesis with it. An organisation publishes a number that others can check. A district official sees which areas are drying, before they burn. And a further hope: that people take this approach to their own places. BMKG's thresholds suit Indonesia and nowhere else, but the idea that a tool should be calibrated for where it is used — and should say what it does not know — travels anywhere.

Ukuran keberhasilan paket ini bukan jumlah unduhan. Ukurannya adalah apakah ada analisis yang tadinya tidak akan pernah terjadi, akhirnya terjadi — karena orang yang perlu menjawab pertanyaannya tidak lagi harus lebih dulu menjadi ahli penginderaan jauh. Seorang dosen memakainya untuk mengajar. Seorang mahasiswa menyelesaikan skripsinya dengannya. Sebuah lembaga menerbitkan angka yang bisa diperiksa orang lain. Seorang pejabat kabupaten melihat kawasan mana yang sedang mengering, sebelum ia terbakar. Dan harapan yang lebih jauh: bahwa orang mengambil pendekatan ini untuk tempat mereka sendiri. Ambang BMKG hanya cocok untuk Indonesia, tetapi gagasan bahwa sebuah perkakas harus dikalibrasi untuk tempat ia dipakai — dan harus mengatakan apa yang tidak diketahuinya — berlaku di mana saja.

1 · What you'll buildYang akan Anda buat

By the end you'll run a single command like this…

Di akhir tutorial, Anda menjalankan satu perintah seperti ini…

earthchange -s deforestation --lat -3.333 --lon 122.25 --radius 6 --map

…and get three things for your chosen area:

…dan mendapat tiga berkas untuk area pilihan Anda:

  • a quick-look PNG of the change,
  • a full-resolution, georeferenced GeoTIFF you can open in QGIS/ArcGIS, and
  • a value-added map (A4 PDF + PNG) with legend, scale bar, statistics and a location inset.

Cloud and shadow are the main source of false positives, and the direction is not random: shadow depresses NIR more than SWIR, so it lowers both NDVI and NBR — it mimics vegetation loss and burn, never gain. Per-pixel cloud masking plus a median over many scenes absorbs most of that, but a median over one clear look simply reproduces whatever the mask missed. Change is therefore only reported where both windows have at least --min-obs cloud-free looks at that pixel (default 2); the excluded share is printed and recorded in stats.clear_observations. Raise it to 3–4 in persistently cloudy areas, --min-obs 1 to disable. What this does not fix: a pixel with many looks that are all shadowed the same way — seasonal terrain shadow, for instance — passes the count test and still biases the result.

Awan dan bayangan adalah sumber utama positif palsu, dan arahnya tidak acak: bayangan menurunkan NIR lebih besar daripada SWIR, sehingga menurunkan NDVI maupun NBR — ia menyerupai kehilangan vegetasi dan kebakaran, tidak pernah pertambahan. Mask awan per piksel plus median banyak scene meredam sebagian besar hal itu, tetapi median dari satu pengamatan bersih hanya mengulang apa pun yang lolos dari mask. Karena itu perubahan hanya dilaporkan bila kedua jendela punya minimal --min-obs pengamatan bebas awan pada piksel tersebut (bawaan 2); porsi yang dikecualikan dicetak dan dicatat di stats.clear_observations. Naikkan ke 3–4 di wilayah yang terus berawan, --min-obs 1 untuk menonaktifkan. Yang belum teratasi: piksel dengan banyak pengamatan yang semuanya terbayang dengan cara sama — misalnya bayangan topografi musiman — lolos uji jumlah dan tetap membiaskan hasil.

Optical scenarios also write a SWIR false-colour quick-look of each source composite — the pre and post imagery the change was measured between — and record every scene that went into them (id, date, cloud %) under images_used in stats.json. A count alone can be neither checked nor reproduced; the ids can. Use --no-preview to skip both.

  • PNG pratinjau perubahan,
  • GeoTIFF resolusi penuh tergeoreferensi yang bisa dibuka di QGIS/ArcGIS, dan
  • peta value-added (A4 PDF + PNG) dengan legenda, skala, statistik, dan inset lokasi.

Skenario optik juga menuliskan pratinjau warna semu SWIR untuk tiap komposit sumber — citra pra dan pasca yang menjadi dasar pengukuran perubahan — serta mencatat setiap scene yang menyusunnya (id, tanggal, % awan) pada images_used di stats.json. Jumlah scene saja tidak bisa diperiksa maupun direproduksi; id-nya bisa. Pakai --no-preview untuk melewati keduanya.

You pick a scenario (what kind of change) and a location (a coordinate). The tool selects the right remote-sensing method automatically. No prior GIS coding required.

Anda memilih skenario (jenis perubahan) dan lokasi (koordinat). Alat memilih metode penginderaan jauh yang tepat secara otomatis. Tanpa perlu pengalaman pemrograman GIS.

smoke-track Which way the wind carried the smoke — indicative forward trajectories. smoke-exposure answers who breathed it; this answers where it went. Air parcels are seeded on the day's hottest FIRMS detections and carried forward on the hourly ERA5 100 m wind field by midpoint (RK2) integration, wrapped as a MovingPandas TrajectoryCollection, and drawn as a fan of threads over the CAMS PM2.5 field they are meant to explain. The output names the districts the parcels crossed and how many crossed each. The fan is the point: a single bold arrow would claim a precision that does not exist, whereas the spread between threads is the honest width of the answer. Kalimantan, 15 September 2019: 60 parcels, median path 365 km in 48 h, crossing Sekadau (42), Ketapang (40) and Sintang (33). ILLUSTRATION, NOT ATTRIBUTION — read this before quoting it. A line drawn from a fire to a city is the most persuasive object a map can contain, and this one does not deserve that much trust. 100 m wind is the best level Earth Engine carries; smoke that lofts into the boundary layer (500–1500 m by afternoon) travels faster and on a different bearing. There is no vertical motion, no plume rise, no mixing depth, no deposition and no dispersion — a parcel here stays at one level forever and never spreads. Crossing a district is not the same as depositing smoke on it. For any claim that has to survive a challenge — who harmed whom, a legal or compliance case — run HYSPLIT or FLEXPART against the receptor points smoke-exposure identifies. Those models exist because this problem is hard. What this is for is showing a general public, honestly labelled, which way the wind was blowing. smoke-track --engine hysplit The same figure, with the physics the caveat asks for. The kinematic engine above says it is an illustration because it has to. This hands the same job to NOAA ARL HYSPLIT, which resolves vertical motion, mixing depth and terrain — and the figure comes out looking the same, deliberately, so a reader who can read one can read the other. What changes is the footer: the "illustration, not attribution" warning is replaced by the model configuration, because it has been earned. Two things make this practical rather than aspirational. The public unregistered HYSPLIT build is not crippled for this purpose — its one restriction is dispersion using forecast meteorology, and everything here is retrospective, so the restriction never binds. And the meteorology needs no account: GDAS1 (1°, 3-hourly, back to December 2004) is on public S3, fetched automatically and cached, ~571 MiB per week of run. --direction backward is the reason to bother. A forward fan from fires is suggestive; a backward trajectory from a receptor answers the question that has to survive challenge — where did the air over this city come from? Receptors are chosen automatically as the districts with the highest CAMS PM2.5 that day, which is the same ranking smoke-exposure reports, so the two scenarios line up and the source↔receptor link stops being hand-waving. Or name them yourself with --receptors "Pontianak,109.33,-0.02; Kuching,110.34,1.55" — explicit rather than geocoded, for the reason smoke-video takes --video-cities that way: a name lookup cannot tell you which places matter for this question. Run both directions on one day and they check each other. Kalimantan, 15 September 2019: forward from the West Kalimantan fires the parcels vent northwest toward Sarawak and the coast; backward from Pontianak, Singkawang, Sambas and Kuching (PM2.5 47–63 µg/m³ that day), 8 of 12 trajectories come in through Ketapang — the district the forward run started from. Two independent calculations, agreeing. Backward from the day's worst districts tells a different story again: those are all in South and Central Kalimantan, and their air arrives from the south-southeast over the Java Sea, never touching the western fires — so 15 September was two disconnected systems, not one plume, which is what smoke-exposure's three-episode structure looks like from the meteorological side. Backward is refused on the kinematic engine on purpose: running that integrator in reverse would look like a source attribution while being no more defensible than the forward fan. Parcels are released at several heights (--track-heights, default 100/500/1500 m AGL) — the spread between levels is real wind shear, not model uncertainty, and it is the thing the single-level kinematic engine cannot show you. How much does the engine actually change the answer? Measured on Kalimantan, 15 September 2019, same 30 seeds, one HYSPLIT release level so the trajectory counts match: the median path runs 416 km against the kinematic 332 km, 25% farther, because 500 m air is not slowed by surface friction the way 100 m air is. The two agree on only 61% of the districts crossed, and the disagreement is one-sided — HYSPLIT reaches seven districts the kinematic run never touches and none in reverse. Those seven are Kota Pontianak, Kota Singkawang, Sambas, Bengkayang and neighbours: the populated northwest coast, which is exactly where smoke-exposure ranks the burden highest. The kinematic run's headline district, Sekadau, falls from first at 25% to fifth at 9%. So the single-level fan is not merely imprecise — it under-reaches in one direction, and that direction is where the people are. Honest scope, still: these are trajectories, not dispersion. A trajectory line does not spread and does not deposit, so crossing a district is still not the same as depositing smoke on it; concentration attribution needs HYSPLIT in dispersion mode or FLEXPART. GDAS1 at 1° does not resolve local terrain flow. You install HYSPLIT yourself — ARL distributes it under a use agreement a person has to read and accept, so the package finds it or explains how to get it, exactly as smoke-video treats ffmpeg. smoke-exposure Who breathed what, for how long — person-days by ISPU class, per district, split by age. A haze map says the air was bad; this says how many people were in it and which of them were children under five or adults over sixty-five, the groups that carry most of the health burden. CAMS PM2.5 is averaged per district per day, classified on the Indonesian ISPU breakpoints, and multiplied by the WorldPop population underneath. The only forward-looking scenario here: fire-history says where it burned, haze says what happened after, this says whether conditions favour burning now. Six components from daily noon weather (ERA5-Land, 11 km): FFMC (litter dryness → ignition), DMC (loose organic layers), DC (deep compact organic matter), ISI (spread rate), BUI (available fuel), FWI (overall intensity). Equations from Van Wagner (1987); the implementation reproduces the published worked example to within 0.007 on all six. DC and BUI lead here, not FWI — and the reason is concrete. On 30 September 2015, at the peak of Indonesia's worst fire disaster in decades, this returns DC 293 but FWI 5.9, because FWI is dominated by ISI, which responds to wind and fine-fuel moisture within hours. Reporting FWI as the headline would have rated that day "Moderate". Peat fire is driven by deep drying, which is what DC tracks. Cumulative by construction: each day depends on the one before, so the run accumulates --spinup days first (default 60; DC has a ~52-day time lag and a shorter run just reports the starting constant). Day-length factors switch to the equatorial convention inside 15° of the equator, because the tabulated Canadian values are for 46°N. drought Drought — rainfall deficit, vegetation health and ENSO state. Three questions kept deliberately separate, because they can disagree and the disagreement is the finding. Is rain missing? A standardized rainfall anomaly from a rainfall product you choose with --rain-source: CHIRPS (gauge-blended, 1981–, but lagging ~5 weeks), ERA5-Land (reanalysis, 1950–, ~8 days), or IMERG/GSMaP (satellite, 1998–, ~1 day). The lag matters more than it sounds — over Central Java in Aug 2026 CHIRPS reported 101% of normal while ERA5, IMERG and GSMaP, all seeing into late July, independently reported 67–71%. Each source carries its own baseline and they are never mixed, because inter-product bias would otherwise masquerade as an anomaly. Computed over a --spi-months window (default 3; use 6–12 for the hydrological framing), scored against the same calendar window in every year of the 1991–2020 normal, and ranked against the whole record. Are plants suffering yet? VCI from MODIS NDVI and TCI from MODIS LST, combined into the VHI (Kogan 1995); below 40 signals drought stress. Is ENSO or the IOD driving it? The Niño 3.4 SST anomaly and the IOD Dipole Mode Index (Saji et al. 1999) from NOAA OISST, on one axis because for Indonesia the two compound; OISST lags only days — so the ENSO panel runs right up to the present even though CHIRPS lags ~5 weeks. Outputs a three-panel figure, a rainfall map (% of the 1991–2020 normal, per pixel), a classified drought-extent map (VHI in Kogan classes at 1 km, with hectares and % of the AOI in each class — this is the answer to where, which an area-average hides) and GeoTIFFs of both fields. Honest scope: the rainfall index is a z-score, not a gamma-fitted SPI — the two agree for ≥3-month windows in the humid tropics but diverge in the dry season, where rainfall is bounded at zero and skewed; it is reported as rainfall_z throughout. Note also that meteorological drought leads agricultural drought by weeks to months, so a big rainfall deficit with a still-healthy VHI means the impact has not landed yet, not that the deficit is harmless. One combined map — --cdi. The three questions above stay separate by default; --cdi also combines them into a single classified field, following the logic of the EDO Combined Drought Indicator: a rainfall deficit alone is Watch, rainfall plus depleted root-zone soil moisture (ERA5-Land) is Warning, and rainfall plus soil plus an actual vegetation response (MODIS VHI) is Alert — the cascade Wilhite & Glantz (1985) described, read off one raster. A fourth class, soil deficit with no rainfall trigger, is kept separate rather than folded into Normal, because it usually means irrigation or storage drawdown rather than drought. Why radar for floods and mining? Optical sensors can't see through clouds — a serious problem during floods and in the wet tropics. Sentinel-1 radar penetrates cloud and works day or night, so it captures change when optical imagery is blind. VH polarisation is most sensitive to land-surface change; VV best separates smooth open water.
Mengapa radar untuk banjir dan tambang? Sensor optik tak bisa menembus awan — masalah serius saat banjir dan di tropis basah. Radar Sentinel-1 menembus awan dan bekerja siang-malam, sehingga menangkap perubahan ketika citra optik buta. Polarisasi VH paling peka pada perubahan permukaan; VV paling baik memisahkan air terbuka.

How clouds are handledCara menangani awan

Optical scenarios never rely on a single image. For each period the tool builds a cloud-masked median composite of many Sentinel-2 scenes: every scene is masked pixel-by-pixel using the Scene Classification (SCL) band, then the median is taken. The result is effectively cloud-free even in cloudy regions. The standalone Sentinel-2 downloader additionally prefers a single scene with ≤10% cloud, falling back to the composite when none exists.

Skenario optik tak pernah bergantung pada satu citra. Untuk tiap periode, alat menyusun median composite bebas awan dari banyak scene Sentinel-2: tiap scene di-mask per-piksel memakai band Scene Classification (SCL), lalu diambil mediannya. Hasilnya praktis bebas awan bahkan di wilayah berawan. Pengunduh Sentinel-2 mandiri juga mengutamakan satu scene dengan awan ≤10%, dan beralih ke composite bila tak ada.

Island heat: poster cerita (--infographic). Menambah poster satu halaman, island_heat_story_<lang>.png: tiga angka tren utama (SST / LST / wet-bulb dalam °C per dekade), grafik tren gabungan, dan puncak wet-bulb tahunan terhadap ambang bahaya 28 °C dan ekstrem 31 °C, lengkap dengan sumber dan batasannya. Pilih --lang id (default), en, atau both. Perlu --island-mode aggregate — mode per-pulau tak punya satu deret untuk diceritakan.
Custom transects (--transects-file). In complex bays the auto-transects can mis-orient. Draw your own cross-shore lines in QGIS, save them as a GeoJSON of LineStrings (first vertex = landward), and pass --transects-file my.geojson — each is intersected with every epoch's shoreline and regressed to a clean m/yr rate (CoastSat-style, MIT-native). On tides: shorelines here come from median composites, which are already tide-averaged (waterline ≈ mean tide). Per-scene tidal correction applies only to per-date imagery, not composites, so it's deliberately not faked.
Transek kustom (--transects-file). Di teluk kompleks, transek otomatis bisa salah arah. Gambar sendiri garis lintas-pantai di QGIS, simpan sebagai GeoJSON LineString (titik pertama = sisi darat), lalu berikan --transects-file my.geojson — tiap transek dipotong dengan garis pantai tiap epoch dan diregresi menjadi laju m/thn yang bersih (ala CoastSat, MIT-native). Soal pasang-surut: garis pantai di sini dari komposit median yang sudah ter-rata-rata terhadap pasang (waterline ≈ muka air rata-rata). Koreksi pasang per-scene hanya berlaku untuk citra per-tanggal, bukan komposit, jadi sengaja tidak dipalsukan.
Hybrid PlanetScope close-up (--planet). For urban-history, free wide-area GHSL locates the single most-changed grid cell, then a small, quota-cheap PlanetScope daily scene (4-band → NDVI works) zooms in at ~3 m. It's quota-safe: search is free, and ordering/downloading only happens with --planet-confirm. Set the change period with --hotspot-from/--hotspot-to and the imagery dates with --planet-pre/--planet-post. Key from $PLANET_API_KEY or ~/.planet.json/~/planet.conf. Without --planet, PlanetScope is never touched.
Close-up hibrida PlanetScope (--planet). Untuk urban-history, GHSL area-luas gratis menemukan sel paling banyak berubah, lalu satu scene harian PlanetScope (4-band → NDVI) meng-close-up pada ~3 m. Hemat kuota: pencarian gratis, unduhan hanya dengan --planet-confirm. Atur periode perubahan dengan --hotspot-from/--hotspot-to dan tanggal citra dengan --planet-pre/--planet-post. Kunci dari $PLANET_API_KEY atau ~/.planet.json/~/planet.conf. Tanpa --planet, PlanetScope tak pernah disentuh.
Flood and burn require dates. They compare a specific event to a baseline, so pass --pre START:END --post START:END (format YYYY-MM-DD:YYYY-MM-DD). Other scenarios have default windows you can still override. For flood, each window is a search range, not a composite — the tool picks a single Sentinel-1 scene from each (the most recent pass, same relative orbit). Keep windows tight (≈2–4 weeks) so each holds a pass but doesn't reach far from the event; place --post to end on/after the flood peak, since it takes the latest pass in the window.
Banjir dan kebakaran wajib tanggal. Keduanya membandingkan kejadian tertentu dengan baseline, jadi berikan --pre START:END --post START:END (format YYYY-MM-DD:YYYY-MM-DD). Skenario lain punya jendela default yang tetap bisa diubah. Untuk banjir, tiap jendela adalah rentang pencarian, bukan komposit — alat memilih satu citra Sentinel-1 dari masing-masing (lintasan terbaru, orbit relatif sama). Buat jendela sempit (≈2–4 minggu) agar tiap jendela berisi satu lintasan namun tidak jauh dari kejadian; atur --post berakhir pada/setelah puncak banjir, karena diambil lintasan terbaru dalam jendela.
Custom SIRAD periods (mining). -s mining builds an R/G/B radar composite from three time periods — blue = activity only in the newest. Set them with --epochs W1,W2,W3 (the same option urban-trend uses); omit it for the default recent years: earthchange -s mining --lat -3.333 --lon 122.25 --epochs 2024-01-01:2024-12-31,2025-01-01:2025-12-31,2026-01-01:2026-06-30
Periode SIRAD kustom (mining). -s mining membangun komposit radar R/G/B dari tiga periode — biru = aktivitas hanya di periode terbaru. Aturlah dengan --epochs W1,W2,W3 (opsi yang sama dengan urban-trend); kosongkan untuk memakai tahun terkini default: earthchange -s mining --lat -3.333 --lon 122.25 --epochs 2024-01-01:2024-12-31,2025-01-01:2025-12-31,2026-01-01:2026-06-30
Alternative methods (e.g. urbanisation). Optical scenarios aren't tied to one index — swap with --method. Sentinel-2 built-up: NDBI (default), UI, BU (=NDBI−NDVI), IBI. Thermal built-up NDISI, EBBI auto-switch to Landsat 8/9 (they need a thermal band Sentinel-2 lacks). Each method has its own default thresholds — tune with --thr/--severe.
Metode alternatif (mis. urbanisasi). Skenario optik tak terikat satu indeks — ganti dengan --method. Built-up Sentinel-2: NDBI (default), UI, BU (=NDBI−NDVI), IBI. Indeks termal NDISI, EBBI otomatis beralih ke Landsat 8/9 (butuh band termal yang tak ada di Sentinel-2). Tiap metode punya ambang default — setel dengan --thr/--severe.
Pre-2015 baselines (--sensor landsat) & water masking. Sentinel-2 starts ~2015; for older baselines — e.g. deforestation since the 1980s — add --sensor landsat to use the Landsat SR archive (back to 1984 via Landsat 5 TM; L7 skipped), 30 m. Works for the optical scenarios on both backends (GEE is most reliable for the old archive). For NDVI-based analyses, sea/rivers/lakes are masked out by default (MNDWI>0 both dates) so water isn't counted as vegetation change — essential on islands & coasts; disable with --no-water-mask (the water/NDWI scenario is never masked). On MPC, Sentinel-2 composites are now harmonised across the 2022 processing-baseline change, so long-baseline NDVI isn't biased.
Baseline pra-2015 (--sensor landsat) & masking air. Sentinel-2 baru ada sejak ~2015; untuk baseline lama — mis. deforestasi sejak 1980-an — tambahkan --sensor landsat untuk memakai arsip reflektansi Landsat (sejak 1984 via Landsat 5 TM; L7 dilewati), 30 m. Berlaku untuk skenario optik di kedua backend (GEE paling andal untuk arsip lama). Untuk analisis berbasis NDVI, laut/sungai/danau (MNDWI>0 kedua tanggal) di-mask otomatis agar air tak terhitung sebagai perubahan vegetasi — penting di pulau & pesisir; matikan dengan --no-water-mask (skenario water/NDWI tak pernah di-mask). Di MPC, komposit Sentinel-2 kini diselaraskan untuk perubahan processing-baseline 2022 agar NDVI baseline-panjang tak bias.
Three (or more) dates / historical years. Sentinel-2 only starts ~2015 — it can't see 2010. For multi-year growth use -s urban-trend, which runs on Landsat (archive back to 1984) and maps NDBI at three epochs to R/G/B in one image (white = built the whole time, blue = newest growth): earthchange -s urban-trend --lat -6.30 --lon 107.15 --epochs 2010-01-01:2010-12-31,2015-01-01:2015-12-31,2020-01-01:2020-12-31
Tiga (atau lebih) tanggal / tahun historis. Sentinel-2 baru ada sejak ~2015 — tak bisa melihat 2010. Untuk pertumbuhan multi-tahun pakai -s urban-trend yang berbasis Landsat (arsip sejak 1984) dan memetakan NDBI tiga epoch ke R/G/B dalam satu citra (putih = terbangun terus, biru = paling baru): earthchange -s urban-trend --lat -6.30 --lon 107.15 --epochs 2010-01-01:2010-12-31,2015-01-01:2015-12-31,2020-01-01:2020-12-31

Tambahkan --map pada run apa pun untuk membuat lembar peta A4 landscape (PDF + PNG) per produk: basemap OpenStreetMap, layer perubahan, judul, legenda, panel statistik, inset lokasi, grid koordinat, skala, dan panah utara.

8 · Adapt it to your own areaMenyesuaikan untuk area Anda

Pick a locationPilih lokasi

Find coordinates by right-clicking a spot in Google Maps (it copies lat, lon), or from OpenStreetMap. Choose a --radius to match the feature: a few km for a single mine or town, 10–20 km for a flood plain or a fire.

Cari koordinat dengan klik kanan di Google Maps (menyalin lat, lon), atau dari OpenStreetMap. Pilih --radius sesuai objek: beberapa km untuk satu tambang atau kota, 10–20 km untuk dataran banjir atau kebakaran.

Save a named presetSimpan preset bernama

Add reusable places to sites.py and call them with --site:

Tambahkan lokasi yang sering dipakai ke sites.py dan panggil dengan --site:

SITES = {
  "my_area": {
    "label": "My study area",
    "lat": 1.234, "lon": 103.456, "radius_km": 8.0,
    "sentinel2_date": "2026-06-15",
    "sirad_periods": [("2024-01-01","2024-12-31"),
                      ("2025-01-01","2025-12-31"),
                      ("2026-01-01","2026-06-30")],
    "ndvi_pre":  ("2023-01-01","2023-12-31"),
    "ndvi_post": ("2026-01-01","2026-06-30"),
  },
}
earthchange -s deforestation --site my_area --map

Add a brand-new scenarioTambah skenario baru

Most scenarios are one dictionary entry in scenarios.py. To add, say, a moisture-change scenario using NDWI on a custom threshold you only add a registry entry — the generic optical-change engine does the rest:

Kebanyakan skenario hanya satu entri dictionary di scenarios.py. Untuk menambah, misalnya, skenario perubahan kelembapan memakai NDWI dengan ambang khusus, cukup tambahkan satu entri — mesin perubahan optik generik menangani sisanya:

"moisture": {
    "label": "Moisture change (NDWI)",
    "run": _optical("NDWI", "gain", 0.08, 0.20),  # index, direction, thresholds
    "radius": 10.0, "needs": "pre_post",
    "pre":  ("2023-01-01","2023-12-31"),
    "post": ("2025-01-01","2025-12-31"),
    "interpretation": "Greener = wetter.",
},

New spectral indices go in indices.py (a one-line normalised difference). Radar or bespoke methods get a small run_* function in scenarios.py.

Indeks spektral baru masuk ke indices.py (satu baris normalized difference). Metode radar atau khusus memakai fungsi run_* kecil di scenarios.py.

9 · Reproducibility & dataReproduksibilitas & data

  • Data: Copernicus Sentinel-1 (radar) and Sentinel-2 (optical), free and open, courtesy ESA, processed in Google Earth Engine.
  • AOI: a square centred on your coordinate (half-side = radius), axis-aligned in EPSG:4326.
  • Orbit auto-selection: radar scenarios check both ascending and descending orbits and use whichever has imagery in every period.
  • Outputs are regenerable, so results and maps are git-ignored; only code and this tutorial are versioned.
  • Data: Copernicus Sentinel-1 (radar) dan Sentinel-2 (optik), gratis dan terbuka, dari ESA, diproses di Google Earth Engine.
  • AOI: persegi berpusat di koordinat Anda (setengah-sisi = radius), sejajar sumbu di EPSG:4326.
  • Pemilihan orbit otomatis: skenario radar memeriksa orbit ascending dan descending lalu memakai yang punya citra di tiap periode.
  • Keluaran bisa dibuat ulang, jadi hasil dan peta di-git-ignore; hanya kode dan tutorial ini yang diversikan.
